What makes it different

RideMyWork is carpooling, not commercial driving.

Traditional ride-hailing starts when someone requests a vehicle. RideMyWork starts with a real journey already happening. Trip Hosts share empty seats, passengers split the cost, and the platform keeps the experience verified, traceable and community-minded.

How it works

One shared journey, checked from both sides.

The product flow is simple enough for daily commutes and strict enough to keep the platform from becoming informal commercial transport.

FAQ

Clear answers for a new commute habit.

Is RideMyWork a ride-hailing service?

No. RideMyWork connects passengers to existing journeys. Trip Hosts must already have a genuine reason to make the trip.

What is a Trip Host?

A Trip Host is someone already making a journey who has available seats in their vehicle.

Who chooses the contribution?

RideMyWork recommends a contribution based on the journey. The Trip Host chooses the final amount within the platform's allowed limit.

What is a boarding code?

It is a unique trip code that helps confirm the passenger is the right person at pickup.

Can I create recurring trips?

Yes. Recurring commutes are a core part of the product for repeated weekly journeys.
